Wireframes · §19.2 · FE-003, FE-020

Home

Home is a dated page that starts with a note. The left column is writing (composer, pinned, recent); the right column is the day around it (sessions, projects, upcoming). Every block links to its full area. In phases without Notes or Calendar the corresponding blocks are simply absent.
